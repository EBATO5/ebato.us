04 // Starting frequencies

Presets & custom root

Use a custom frequency as root + Current Note
Preset playback behavior For roots below 55 Hz, the lab automatically auditions the lowest octave at or above 55 Hz so you can actually hear it. The root itself remains the exact preset frequency. Use Fundamental or Octave ↓ to return to the exact low-frequency root.

Preset names are user-defined labels, not established medical effects. Very low frequencies may not be reproduced by ordinary speakers or headphones.
