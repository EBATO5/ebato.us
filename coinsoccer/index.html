How to Play

Goal: Advance your three coins up the field and score. First player to 3 or more points wins.

1. Tap/click a coin, pull backward to set direction and power, then release.

2. The other two coins form your gate. Every non-kickoff shot must pass through that gate or possession turns over.

3. The kickoff shot cannot cause a turnover.

4. Easy rules: all three coins are pennies and each goal is worth 1 point. Standard rules: pennies are worth 1 point and the dime is worth 2. Hard rules: only the dime can score; pennies can still be used for passes, gates, and collisions.

5. In VS Computer, AI difficulty defaults to Normal. Use the Easy / Normal / Hard slider to change AI execution and goalkeeper strength. Normal keeps the established standard AI behavior.

6. After a goal, the player who conceded gets the next kickoff unless the goal ends the game.

7. After a completed game, the player who did not kick off that game starts the next one. All human players must press Play New Game before the next game begins.

8. Restart Game restarts the current game with the same kickoff player. Main Menu returns to match selection.

9. Human goalkeepers start in manual mode. Drag your keeper left/right and it stays where you leave it. Toggle Auto Goalie on to make your keeper patrol automatically; grabbing it still temporarily overrides auto movement. The computer's own keeper remains automatic.

10. On desktop, right-click while pulling a coin to cancel the shot.

Online: Create a lobby, share the 6-character code, and both players always see their own goal at the bottom. Both players must ready up after a completed game. Each player controls their own Auto Goalie preference.
