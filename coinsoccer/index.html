How to Play

Goal: Advance your three coins up the field and score. First player to 7 or more points wins.

1. Tap/click a coin, pull backward to set direction and power, then release.

2. The other two coins form your gate. Every non-kickoff shot must pass through that gate or possession turns over.

3. The kickoff shot cannot cause a turnover.

4. Any coin can score. A penny goal is worth 1 point and a dime goal is worth 2 points. In Easy Mode, all three coins are pennies, so every goal is worth 1 point.

5. After a goal, the player who conceded gets the next kickoff unless the goal ends the match.

6. Your goalkeeper moves automatically. Grab and drag it left/right whenever you want manual control. Let go and auto movement resumes.

7. On desktop, right-click while pulling a coin to cancel the shot.

Online: Create a lobby, share the 6-character code, and both players always see their own goal at the bottom.
