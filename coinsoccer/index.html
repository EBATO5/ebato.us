How to Play

Goal: Advance your three coins up the field and score. First player to 3 or more points wins.

1. Tap/click a coin, pull backward to set direction and power, then release.

2. The other two coins form your gate. Every non-kickoff shot must pass through that gate or possession turns over.

3. The kickoff shot cannot cause a turnover.

4. Tap Difficulty to choose the game rules: Easy uses three pennies worth 1 point each. Normal uses pennies worth 1 and a dime worth 2. Hard allows only the dime to score. Rule changes apply when the difficulty window closes.

5. Difficulty does not change the computer AI. VS Computer uses the established standard AI behavior.

6. Opening any menu pauses the game completely until the menu closes.

7. After a goal, the player who conceded gets the next kickoff unless the goal ends the game.

8. After a completed game, the player who did not kick off that game starts the next one. All human players must press Play New Game before the next game begins.

9. Restart Game restarts the current game with the same kickoff player. Main Menu returns to match selection.

10. Human goalkeepers start in manual mode. Drag your keeper left/right and it stays where you leave it. Toggle Auto Goalie on if you want automatic patrol. The computer's own keeper remains automatic.

11. On desktop, right-click while pulling a coin to cancel the shot.

Online: Create a lobby, share the 6-character code, and both players always see their own goal at the bottom. Both players must ready up after a completed game. Each player controls their own Auto Goalie preference.
